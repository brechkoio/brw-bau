# Rules and Regulations

## Site safety

- Hard hats, safety glasses, and protective footwear are mandatory anywhere on the site.
- Being on site under the influence of alcohol or drugs is prohibited.
- Only personnel who have completed the relevant training may operate machinery and equipment.
- Any injury or accident must be reported immediately to the foreman or the person responsible for workplace safety.
- Work in areas marked off with warning tape is prohibited without the site manager's approval.

## Working hours

- The workday starts and ends with the time entry in the app — don't forget to tap "Start" and "End".
- The lunch break doesn't count as paid time.
- Notify the foreman in advance about lateness or absence.

## Site order

- Clean up your work area and tools after finishing work.
- Smoking is only allowed in designated areas.
- Dispose of trash and construction waste only in the designated containers.

_This is an example of basic rules. Management may add to or change this list._
